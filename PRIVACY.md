# Ferret privacy policy

Ferret is built so that nothing about you or your traffic leaves your iPhone.

- **No accounts, analytics, telemetry or tracking.** Ferret has no servers.
- **Traffic stays on its normal path.** Capture uses a VPN configuration that runs
  entirely on the device. Packets go straight to where your apps were sending them.
  Ferret doesn't route them through any server, and DNS keeps using your network's resolvers.
- **Captures stay on the device.** Packets are stored in Ferret's App Group container,
  capped at the size you choose, and can be deleted in one tap. They only leave the
  phone if you export them yourself.
- **Safety Snoot** connects to a few well-known services to test the network you're on:
  captive.apple.com, www.apple.com, www.google.com, www.cloudflare.com, en.wikipedia.org,
  and DNS lookups for one.one.one.one, dns.google and dns.quad9.net. It sends no personal data.
- **Location and local network permissions** are only used to read the Wi-Fi network's
  name and security type and to list Bonjour services during Safety Snoot. Nothing is stored or sent.
- **Tips** are handled by the App Store. Ferret never sees your payment details.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Austin-Higgins/ferret.
